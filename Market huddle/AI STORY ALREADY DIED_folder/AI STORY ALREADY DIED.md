*   **Financial Accident Risk:** If a major systemic risk event occurs (financial accident), the Fed may aggressively cut rates, potentially leading to a long-end bond rally and yield crash, though supply issues complicate this.

3. Actionable Takeaways:
*   **Audit Concentration Risk:** Review portfolios against individual stock performance, not just index data. Be aware that holding broad index funds may provide false security if the market is dominated by overvalued tech stocks.
*   **Buy Volatility Protection:** With VIX near historical lows and high individual asset vol, consider purchasing cheap tail insurance/straddles on low-volatility names like Google. Do not rely solely on option pricing; buy volatility early to manage potential regime changes.

*   **Watch Credit Spreads:** Avoid private equity credit instruments (e.g., BDCs) and bank sectors that may suffer from deteriorating credit margins due to interest rate volatility.
*   **Prepare for Earnings Miss:** Given that highest growth rates are already priced into AI stocks, position for potential earnings failures as the primary trigger for a broader market downturn.
