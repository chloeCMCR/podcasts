Bilal Hafiz argues that the global economy is entering a "Spending Super Cycle" and "Global Growth Long Cycle" extending through 2030, driven by unprecedented regional investment in AI, defense, and climate across Asia, Europe, and the Americas. Despite macroeconomic concerns regarding US interest rates, geopolitical conflict (Middle East, Russia-Ukraine), and inflation, equities remain undervalued globally with robust earnings growth.     *   **South Korea:** Trading at ~5x forward earnings with >100% earnings growth driven by AI/Memory.

    *   Investors should ignore news and focus on asset price reactions; stocks have been digesting uncertainty without a meaningful index decline.
