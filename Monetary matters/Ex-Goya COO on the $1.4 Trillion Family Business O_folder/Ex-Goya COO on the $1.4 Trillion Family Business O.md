    *   **Health & Nutrition:** Rise of "healthy snacking," high protein/fiber products, and adaptation to new drug classes like GLP-1s (users eating lower caloric volume but needing more protein). Mega-CPGs are pivoting towards this via packaging changes, size reductions, and cleaner labels.

Buyers include large sponsors (sovereign wealth funds), strategic companies, and other family offices.
*   **Manufacturing Renaissance:** Manufacturing is returning to the U.S., focusing on cleaner, safer, and more efficient facilities. Paying workers a living wage reduces turnover and long-term costs despite perceived higher immediate widget costs.

3. Actionable Takeaways
*   **For Investors:** Diversify into lower middle market family-owned businesses rather than solely chasing high-tech mega-capitals.
