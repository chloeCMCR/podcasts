Copper sentiment is hot due to AI trade speculation, but Jared notes gold/silver/platinum as better long-term alternatives.
*   **Private Credit Liquidity:** Private credit and private equity markets face an unwinding that will take much longer than public markets due to a lack of liquidity; this bearish view remains unchanged despite recent media coverage.
*   **The Awesome Portfolio Strategy:** Diversifying across stocks, bonds, gold, cash, and real estate (roughly 1:4:1:2:3 ratios) cuts volatility in half compared to S&P 500. Worst-case drawdown drops from ~40% to ~12%, protecting against psychological panic selling despite potentially lower compounding returns.
*   **Productivity & AI:** AI is financing tech with record debt loads (e.g., Google) unlike the equity-fueled Dot-com bubble; high CapEx demand may be inflationary rather than deflationary.

3. Actionable Takeaways:
*   **Reallocate to Bonds:** Increase exposure to bonds as current yields offer a "deals" opportunity for long-term holdings (3-5 years) and act as a hedge against stock volatility.
*   **Adopt Volatility-Conscious Allocation:** Shift from an 80/20 S&P 500 strategy to an equal-weighted multi-asset portfolio to reduce emotional stress and prevent "tapping out" during corrections.
*   **Monitor Labor Metrics:** Watch payroll data closely (negative 50k jobs vs. positive 100k) as a trigger for the Fed to pivot to dovish policy, which could boost gold prices near $4,000 support.
*   **Avoid Retail Momentum in AI:** Steer clear of concentrated bets on over-valued sectors like semiconductors where retail positioning is low but institutional leverage may unwind; prioritize companies with tangible revenue growth data over hype-driven narratives.
*   **Manage Sentiment Bias:** Counter the "bond market bearish" media narrative with a contrarian approach, while maintaining discipline to hold investments through expected market volatility rather than selling during drawdowns.
