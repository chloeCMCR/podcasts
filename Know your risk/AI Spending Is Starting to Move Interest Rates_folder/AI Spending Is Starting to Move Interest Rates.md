# AI Spending Is Starting to Move Interest Rates
**Podcast:** Know your risk

## AI Executive Summary
### 1. Core Thesis
The episode argues that the current market environment is driven by a disconnect between technological hype and underlying economic fundamentals, specifically concerning AI capital expenditure (CAPEX) and fiscal dominance. While tech stocks are rallying due to cross-sector flows, investors are advised to remain cautious regarding AI growth sustainability; if major companies reduce CAPEX, inflation forecasts will likely adjust downward, potentially lowering bond yields. The hosts caution against complacency in high-yield debt markets due to hyperscaler paper issuance and suggest that geopolitical instability (oil transit costs) and structural government financing needs will create a challenging "fiscal dominance" scenario where rates may not normalize until further market conviction breakouts occur.

### 2. Key Arguments & Data
*   **Market Performance:** S&P down ~0.5% (vs. Equal Weight up ~0.5%). Small caps flat. Mag 7 down ~1%. NASDAQ down ~1% despite strong AI hype. Oil up ~3% due to geopolitical supply constraints (Iran/India). Gold up ~0.4%, Silver down ~1.5%.
*   **AI & Tech Dynamics:** FT report indicates OpenAI revenue revisions from $70B to $50B estimates, signaling a potential drop in token pricing and capital burn rate. Nvidia is down 3% with earnings projections trading at under 10x 2029 estimates. AI trade volatility is cited as the primary inflation driver; slowing CAPEX = rate cut expectation.
*   **Geopolitics & Energy:** Iran transit costs for oil shipments have escalated (up to 5-10x pre-war pricing) with crew bonuses reaching $100k/month per captain due to targeted attacks on vessels. Oil prices remain high (~$110+) despite Trump's comment that he will wait before hitting Iran, intended to stabilize markets but failed to drop prices significantly.
*   **Economic Policy & Debt:** Fiscal dominance is viewed as inevitable; governments will override fiscal constraints. Hyperscalers are issuing significant debt (e.g., Nvidia raising $40B at ~8-9% yields) causing credit spreads to widen and high-yield paper supply to increase. The US missed the opportunity to "lock in" long-term rates at 4% historically, now facing a steep curve environment.
*   **Social & Behavioral:** Discussion of gambling/sports betting legality vs. addiction risks (moral hazard). Personal anecdote regarding employee lost wages from sports bets. Debate on Libertarian views being challenged by the societal costs of gambling and dopamine-driven behavior.

### 3. Actionable Takeaways
*   **Monitor CAPEX Signals:** Watch for corporate announcements indicating a slowdown in AI expenditure, as this would logically drive down inflation forecasts and bond yields (potential catalyst for TLT positions).
*   **Diversify with Hedges:** Current market conditions lack conviction breakouts; maintain tail risk coverage and hedge both upside potential and downside drawdown risks.
*   **Beware of Credit Spreads:** High-yield credit is widening due to paper issuance by hyperscalers, not necessarily signaling a macro turn yet, but requires scrutiny regarding debt servicing capacity (e.g., $3 billion/year for one tranche).
*   **Watch Oil/Geo Flows:** The economic impact of rising transit costs on energy margins remains a critical factor; do not ignore the supply chain implications despite geopolitical noise.
*   **Evaluate Growth Valuations:** Do not chase tech valuations without considering 2029 earnings potential or revenue growth rates that match the cost of capital, acknowledging the risk of "bailouts" or government intervention in public-private partnerships.
