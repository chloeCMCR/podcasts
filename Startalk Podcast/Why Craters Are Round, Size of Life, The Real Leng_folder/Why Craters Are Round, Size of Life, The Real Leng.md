*   **Lunar Crater Formation:** All lunar craters are perfect circles because meteoroids impact at such high speeds that kinetic energy exceeds the object's binding energy. Computer simulations from the 1970s proved that if found, the original asteroid would have been vaporized rather than buried.
*   **Sidereal vs. Solar Day:** Earth completes one rotation relative to fixed stars in exactly 23 hours, 56 minutes, and 4 seconds (sidereal day). The solar day is 24 hours because Earth must rotate an additional ~1 degree to account for its orbit around the Sun during that rotation (~360° / 365 days).

Due to tidal friction from the Moon, Earth's rotation slows, requiring leap seconds to remain synchronized with atomic time (~24 added since the early 70s).

*   **Square-Cube Law in Biology:** Strength scales with cross-sectional area ($r^2$), while mass scales with volume ($r^3$). As size increases, the surface must support disproportionately more weight. This explains why elephants have thick legs but insects can maintain thin limbs to support their own weight relative to their strength.
*   **Surface Tension:** In macroscopic environments (human scale), gravity dominates over surface tension. In microscopic environments (insect scale), surface tension dominates, allowing organisms to walk on water or drink from a bead of fluid without it spreading.
*   **Scaling Limits for Life:** Biological signaling is limited by the speed of light; neurons cannot transmit signals fast enough across a galactic-sized organism, making macroscopic bodies functionally impossible. Conversely, sub-molecular sizes are impossible due to quantum chemistry and molecular binding limits.

*   **Biological Contextualization:** Recognize the physical constraints of size when analyzing animal physiology; appreciate why large land animals require robust skeletal structures while microscopic organisms do not face weight-borne stress.
