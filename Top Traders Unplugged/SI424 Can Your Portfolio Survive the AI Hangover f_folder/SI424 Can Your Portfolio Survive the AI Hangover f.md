The hosts argue that investors must move beyond traditional reliance on negative correlation or "60-40" diversification to build portfolios capable of withstanding multiple adverse economic scenarios, such as AI bubble unwinds, fiscal dominance, or credit market instability.

**2. Key Arguments & Data**

*   **AI Boom & Bust:** A report by Dario Peri (styled as a "Citrini-like" look back) argues the AI boom will unwind post-2030 due to capital depreciation vs. depreciation, citing circular financing flows and high CAPEX without corresponding GDP growth. Alan Dunn suggests this ignores the multiplier effect on construction/infrastructure and the risk of increased debt levels during a potential bust.
*   **Trend Following Performance:** The Stockton Trend Index is down 1.09% monthly but up 5.15% YTD; S&P CTA Index down 1.25% monthly, up 14.21% YTD; MSCI World up 74 basis points vs. US/Europe excluding North America down 1.18%.

A perfect negative correlation period (Dec 21 – Sep 22) showed equities down 20% while bonds fell 60%, yet daily correlation was near zero.
*   **Portfolio Resilience:** The hosts argue resilience is a measurable component beyond risk/return, defined by: moderate growth reliance, independent bets (not just asset class count), inflation protection, and adaptive/convex strategies (CTAs/MACRO).

**3. Actionable Takeaways**
*   **Diversify Beyond Correlation:** Do not rely on bond/equity negative correlation as a primary diversifier; instead focus on building "independent bets" within the portfolio structure to handle scenarios like high debt or inflationary shocks.
*   **Integrate Trend Following for Convexity:** Use trend-following strategies to provide resilience and protection during major equity drawdowns (e.g., 2008, COVID) rather than trying to predict downside risk with traditional hedges; accept opportunity costs as a trade-off for long-term compounding safety.
*   **Scenario Planning Over Prediction:** Avoid over-reliance on specific economic forecasts (like a "Peri-like" AI unwind scenario); instead prepare portfolios that withstand multiple adverse outcomes including fiscal dominance, supply shocks, and high-yield debt crises.
*   **Monitor Credit Systemically:** Keep close watch on credit spreads and high-yield sectors for systemic shifts, particularly if major names like Arenae Capital show significant underperformance or liquidity issues.
*   **Build Adaptive Resilience:** Score portfolios based on resilience components (inflation protection, growth moderation) rather than just volatility reduction; be willing to hold lower-performing assets in specific scenarios that offer portfolio stability over long-term periods.
