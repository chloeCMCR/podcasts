    *   **Shifting Capital Dynamics:** The AI era has turned once cash-rich companies (Microsoft, Google, Amazon) into capital-intensive entities with significantly higher depreciation costs and potentially negative cash flows due to massive infrastructure spending.

*   **Supply Chain and Trade Volatility:**
    *   **Tariff Unpredictability:** The primary threat to importers is not necessarily high rates, but extreme volatility; the US tariff code saw 53 changes in a single 52-week period, leaving companies with almost no planning time.

    *   **Geopolitical Disruptions:** The Strait of Hormuz is effectively seeing a collapse in container ship transit; ships are increasingly routing around the southern tip of Africa to avoid Red Sea threats, which has spiked insurance costs by 10% to 15% per voyage.
    *   **The Rise of Traceability Requirements:** Global regulations (such as EU anti-deforestation laws) are moving toward hyper-granularity, requiring companies to provide GPS coordinates and specific sub-component origins, creating a massive technological burden on traditional supply chain management.
